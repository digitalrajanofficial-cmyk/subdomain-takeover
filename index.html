THE CONCEPT

How the risk happens

01

DNS points outward

A subdomain has a CNAME or other record pointing to a hosted service.

02

Resource is removed

The external resource is deleted, but the DNS record is left behind.

03

Review and remediate

Inventory records, verify ownership, and remove stale DNS references promptly.

Use responsibly

This demonstration performs no DNS resolution, service enumeration, or resource claiming. Only assess domains you own or are explicitly authorized to test.
